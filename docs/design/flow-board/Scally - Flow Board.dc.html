4Launch animation

The mark from 3c doesn't perform — it resolves, then gets out of the way. Brackets land from slightly open, hold for a beat with the pixel, then keep opening until they pass the screen edge. The splash clears before the app is shown — Import never appears behind the mark. 2.2 s end to end, and nothing about it is a loading spinner: it plays once while the app is actually cold-starting, and is cut short the moment the UI is ready. Looping here so you can watch it.
