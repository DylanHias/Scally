CHANGES MADE BY SCALLY
The published PyTorch weights were converted to Core ML packages for on-device
inference on Apple silicon. Conversion casts the weights to float16 and traces
each network at a fixed 256x256 tile size. The networks' arithmetic is
otherwise unaltered. The RealPLKSR conversion was verified against the
author's own ONNX export and agrees to 6.3e-6 in the worst pixel; its float16
Core ML package sits roughly 1% of output range from the float32 PyTorch
reference on photographic input.

ARCHITECTURES
ESRGAN / RRDBNet, from "ESRGAN: Enhanced Super-Resolution Generative
Adversarial Networks" (arXiv:1809.00219).
RealPLKSR, from "Partial Large Kernel CNNs for Efficient Super-Resolution"
(arXiv:2404.11848).
MoSR, as implemented by the neosr project
(https://github.com/neosr-project/neosr).
